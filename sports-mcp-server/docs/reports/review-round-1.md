# Review round 1: sports-mcp-server

Reviewer: independent audit against `docs/SPEC.md`, 2026-10-04 (ESPN data of that day, live). All findings below were
reproduced by me against the running container (`sports-mcp`, 127.0.0.1:8767) and/or raw ESPN JSON.

## Verdict: CHANGES REQUIRED

No BLOCKER. 3 MAJOR, 12 MINOR, 4 NIT. The data path is largely correct (scores, home/away, records, odds sign and
favorite, linescores, scoring plays, team stats, schedule W/L, standings values all matched raw ESPN in every league I
compared). The MAJOR items are a wrong season label, missing upcoming fixtures for soccer, and an unbounded-by-bytes
cache that can exceed the container memory limit.

## Verified OK (brief)

- Layering: no `httpx`/socket/urllib.request import outside `espn_client.py`; one broad `except Exception` (`server._instrumented`, intentional, maps to a safe message); 3 `noqa`/`type: ignore` all justified.

## Deviations assessment (implementer's 14)

1. `lines_basis: closing` extra value: acceptable and better than the spec; rendered and described honestly.
2. 12 MiB cap vs "e.g. 8 MiB": acceptable (college standings are ~6 MB); but see R1-03 for the memory consequence.
3. Default 100-game scoreboard cap: acceptable, but R1-13 (no way past 100).
4. `groups=50` for college basketball: acceptable and necessary (verified 15 vs 92 and 4 vs 131 games); FBS needs no `groups` (verified).
5. Win probability only in/after game: acceptable.
6. Pre-game `team_stats` omitted: acceptable (conservative reading of the season-vs-game rule).
7. No basketball scoring plays: acceptable, documented.
8. Standings truncated at 100 rows with hint: acceptable; hint and group list work (CFB 8 of 12 groups).
9. SDK error prefix / extra args ignored: acceptable (see R1-17 for the verbose validation text).
10. `get_odds` status `{state, detail}`, `draw_moneyline`: acceptable.
11. Spread normalised to favorite handicap: acceptable and verified correct in all sports checked.
12. httpx logging muted: acceptable.
13. `/` and `?` excluded from team/group text: acceptable (message text slightly wrong, R1-18).
14. Phase 2 absent: acceptable, registry has no individual sports.
